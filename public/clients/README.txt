Logo files for the client/partner showcase on the B2B pages.

Naming convention: use the client key from src/config/b2bFacts.ts as the filename.
  intercruises.png
  cultural-habits.png
  lemon-printers.png
  gs-bikes.png

Recommended format: SVG or transparent PNG, min 400 px wide.
Source: logo must come from the client or its official brand page.
Before dropping a logo here, set permission: "confirmed" in b2bFacts.ts
(a WhatsApp or email OK from the client is enough).

The logos are served from /clients/<filename> at runtime.
