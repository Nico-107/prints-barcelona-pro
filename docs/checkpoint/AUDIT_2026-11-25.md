## H. Market research — is €9,90 too low, and how to show delivery

- **Competitors are 2.5–6× more expensive for one small part, shipping included.** All3DP tested one PLA bike phone mount: Craftcloud $37.40 ($29.40 + $8 shipping), PCBWay $43.72, Shapeways $65.00, Xometry $68.54, Protolabs Network $90 (minimum-order surcharge). Dimension3D: €9,90 pickup / €14,80–15,80 delivered. Source: all3dp.com/2/best-cheap-3d-printing-services/
- **Extra costs are the #1 checkout killer:** Baymard — 40 % abandon because extra costs (shipping, tax, fees) were too high; 12 % because they could not see the total upfront; 17 % because the site had errors. Source: baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate-statistics
- **Price–quality inference:** buyers lean on price as a quality signal mainly when quality is hard to judge before buying (Rao 2005 review; Gneezy, Gneezy & Lauga, JMR 2014). A custom print is hard to judge beforehand, so an *implausible* price hurts trust. That is exactly the student's reaction: €9,90 for a 35 cm model looked wrong.
- **Conclusion used in MASTER_PROMPT:** €9,90 as the entry price is not the problem and stays (pickup minimum; "desde 9,90 €" on local pages). What hurts is (1) prices that look broken (B3/B4/B8 underpricing, ~0 g), (2) shipping that appears late or with a different number (B1), (3) dead ends (B2/B4/B6/B7). Delivery is shown as one final number per option, before Buy, and the same number goes to Stripe. On non-local pages the price includes delivery ("desde 14,90 € con envío"). Raising the minimum would be a separate experiment after the checkpoint, not a guess.
